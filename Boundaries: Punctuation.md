# SAT Punctuation Guide with Examples

---

## 1. Commas (,)

**Use to:**

- **Separate list items**\
  Example: I bought apples, oranges, and bananas.
- **Separate nonessential elements**\
  Example: The Eiffel Tower, a famous landmark in Paris, attracts millions of
  tourists.
- **Link dependent clauses to independent clauses**\
  Example: While I was sleeping, my cat knocked over the vase.
- **Link independent clauses with a coordinating conjunction (FANBOYS)**\
  Example: I wanted to go for a walk, but it started raining.

**Do NOT use commas to:**

- **Split a subject and a verb**\
  ❌ Incorrect: Mountain goats, are very nimble.\
  ✅ Correct: Mountain goats are very nimble.\
  ✅ Correct with nonessential phrase: Mountain goats, which are only distantly
  related to domestic goats, are very nimble.\
  _Explanation:_ A comma shouldn't separate the subject from its verb unless
  setting off a nonessential phrase.
- **Come before unnecessary words**\
  ❌ Incorrect: The chicken crossed, to the other side of the road.\
  ❌ Incorrect: The chicken crossed to the other side, of the road.\
  ✅ Correct: The chicken crossed to the other side of the road.
- **Separate items in a list of two**\
  ❌ Incorrect: Learning archery requires skill, and practice.\
  ✅ Correct: Learning archery requires skill and practice.

---

## 2. Semicolons (;)

**Use to:**

- **Link independent clauses without a conjunction**\
  Example: I love pizza; my brother prefers pasta.
- **Separate list items that already contain commas**\
  Example: We visited Paris, France; Rome, Italy; and Berlin, Germany.

**Tip:** Only use semicolons when necessary; otherwise, use commas.

---

## 3. Colons (:)

**Use to:**

- **Introduce explanations**\
  Example: She failed the test for one reason: she didn’t study.
- **Introduce lists**\
  Example: I need three things from the store: milk, eggs, and bread.

**Rule:** The clause before a colon must be complete.

---

## 4. Dashes (—)

**Use to:**

- **Separate nonessential elements**\
  Example: There were three winners—the youngest, the tallest, and the
  fastest—who received prizes.\
  Example: The winners—the youngest, the tallest, and the fastest—stood on
  stage.

**Do NOT use dashes for other purposes** (unless stylistically for emphasis).

---

## Tips for SAT

- **Semicolon check:** Both sides must be independent clauses.
- **Colon check:** Must follow a complete sentence.
- **Comma check:** Ensure it’s necessary and correctly placed.
- **Nonessential elements:** Use commas, dashes, or parentheses.
