# Boundaries: Supplements

Nonessential information about supplements can be separated from the rest of a
sentence using three types of punctuation:

- **Commas (,)**
- **Parentheses ()**
- **Dashes (—)**

**Rule:** The same type of punctuation must appear **before and after** the
nonessential element.

- ❌ Wrong: Supplements (which are popular, —can be risky).
- ✅ Correct: Supplements (which are popular) can be risky.
- ✅ Correct: Supplements —which are popular— can be risky.
