### **Independent clause**

✓ A complete idea ✓ Can stand alone as a sentence

### **Dependent clause**

✗ An incomplete idea ✗ Cannot stand alone as a sentence

---

### **How to link clauses**

#### **1. End punctuation (. ? !)**

- Used **only to end** an independent clause
- **Not** used to link clauses

#### **2. Coordination (linking two independent clauses)**

- Use **comma + FANBOYS** _for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so_ **Example:** A, but
  B.
- Or use just a **coordinating conjunction** if no comma is needed in short
  sentences.

#### **3. Subordination (linking a dependent + independent clause)**

- Use a **subordinating conjunction** _although, because, since, while, when,
  after, before, if, etc._
- **Comma rule:**

  - Dependent clause **first** → use a comma: **Because it rained, we stayed
    inside.**
  - Independent clause **first** → **no comma:** **We stayed inside because it
    rained.**

#### **4. Semicolon ( ; )**

- Links **two independent clauses**
- No conjunction needed **Example:** A; B.

---

### **How to spot clause-linking questions on the SAT**

- Choices add/remove **commas + FANBOYS**
- Choices change **end punctuation** (. ? !)
- A choice uses a **semicolon**
- Choices switch between dependent ↔ independent structures
